# Operations handbook (excerpt)

## Client billing
Only Karan in Finance knows how to generate the milestone-based billing reports from the
legacy invoicing tool. There is no written documentation for this process yet.

## Escalation policy
Any client escalation that copies a CEO must be acknowledged by a partner within 24 hours
and a written recovery plan sent within 5 working days.

## Proposal standards
Every proposal must include: a quantified ROI estimate, at least one relevant case study,
a risk and assumptions section, and a milestone-based payment schedule.
