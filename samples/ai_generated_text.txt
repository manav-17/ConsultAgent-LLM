A short history of modern AI (draft written by an AI assistant)

The Transformer architecture was introduced by Google researchers in 2017 in the paper
"Attention Is All You Need", and it now underpins most large language models.

OpenAI was founded in 2016 by a group that included Sam Altman and Elon Musk, and it
released ChatGPT to the public in November 2022.

Python, the most popular language for machine learning, was created by James Gosling and
first released in 1991.

On the security side, the Log4Shell vulnerability (CVE-2021-44228) in Apache Log4j received
the maximum CVSS score of 10.0 when it was disclosed in December 2021.

The Eiffel Tower, often used as a landmark in computer vision datasets, was completed in 1899.
